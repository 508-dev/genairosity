# Software freedom, in practice.

Genairosity is a 508.dev initiative to put useful software within everyone's reach.
We research proprietary workflows and build independent, free and open-source
alternatives where that effort will help. We also contribute to existing FOSS projects.

## Free from end to end

Source code, server components, and the ability to self-host belong in the open.
AGPL-3.0-or-later is our preferred license. Commercial use remains permitted by our
FOSS licenses; the initiative itself does not require a business case or profitability.

## Independent work, documented evidence

We use public documentation and lawfully observed behavior to write original functional
specifications. Sources and uncertainties are recorded. A maintainer reviews the spec
before implementation begins. We exclude proprietary source, unlicensed assets,
decompilation, and circumvention from our initial process.

A documented process is not a blanket legal guarantee. Doubtful inputs need review.
[Read the research policy](https://github.com/508-dev/genairosity/blob/main/docs/clean-room.md).

## Good enough for everyday life

Performance, accessible design, and approachable workflows are core requirements.
Rust is preferred where appropriate. Linux is a first-class desktop platform;
Android is the mobile priority where applicable. Cross-platform support follows the scope.

## Build where the gap is

Research existing free software first. Strong existing projects deserve our effort.
We choose a bounded workflow and a useful outcome, not a feature checklist for its own sake.

## People choose the work

Anyone can suggest or contribute. Maintainers accept and register projects. Initial
priorities follow research, interest, and available time. AI assistance is optional;
any model work is started locally by a human using their tools of choice. If a project
needs AI features, it should support configurable services and local model endpoints.

## Honest about progress

Candidates are investigations. Registered repositories report their actual stage.
“Usable” means a declared scope works, not full feature parity. Software comes with
no warranty. Names of reference products identify research subjects, not affiliations.
